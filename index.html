Shikayat Saathi

Faced a bribe demand, a refused FIR or rude behaviour from police in Delhi? Find the right place to complain, in 5 steps.

  1. Pick what happened
  2. Collect your proof
  3. See where to file
  4. Write it in your own words
  5. Get a formal complaint letter
  • No login
  • No officer profiles
  • Every route has a source

Prototype · Delhi only · Routes checked 30 Sep 2026. Confirm on the official site before you file.

Step 1 of 5 · What happened

Only what you type in step 4 is sent to OpenAI, to write your letter. This app does not save it, and nothing is posted publicly.
